CASTLE INTERIOR — THE SON'S CELL AND REUNION

Scene group: Castle_SonRescue
The barred cell occupies the back of the final storeroom. Existing barrels,
pillars and the two wooden entry barricades are retained. The same
CastleGateKeyPickup reference is used by the earlier gate and this cell.
Unlocking either door never consumes or replaces the key.

PLAY
Enter the final room to see the son's greeting. Look at the cell door within
3.2 metres and press E. Without the original key, the cell remains locked.
With the key, the lock sounds and the door swings inward over 2.2 seconds.
Walk through the open door, look at the son and press E to talk.
E advances dialogue; Esc closes it and restores the player's controls.
If the optional family drawing was collected, two extra reunion lines appear.
The axe lowers during the conversation and stays lowered for the finale.

After the final line, the son stays in place and the screen immediately begins
a smooth three-second fade to black, followed by the unchanged REUNITED text.
No exit walk or stepping aside is required. The axe and movement stay disabled
during the fade.
Click Continue exploring or press E/Enter to dismiss the ending and restore
normal movement and the axe. The son remains safe in the unlocked cell. The scene
is not reloaded and inventory, open gates and collected clues are retained.
This is a short reunion ending, not a separate escort level or a new scene.
Dialogue is on-screen text; no recorded speech is included.

CHARACTER AND ANIMATION
The existing Synty PolygonGeneric peasant male is scaled to 1.3 metres with
facial-hair attachments disabled and a slightly enlarged head. Its scene child
is named Son visual — replaceable. It uses a dedicated URP material copy.
The existing Kevin Iglesias HumanM Idle01 clip supplies a relaxed idle.
Root motion is disabled; the exit-walk state and waypoint have been removed.
Original character, materials, animation clips and packages are not modified.

ASSETS AND SOUND
Cell bars and door are original primitive geometry. Bedding reuses the project's
linen material and bowl prefab. Lock Unlock and Door Open 1 are reused from the
installed Free Fantasy SFX Pack by TomMusic. No new packages were installed.

REMOVE OR DISABLE
Disable Castle_SonRescue to hide the entire cell, son, light and interface.
Disabling during dialogue or the ending restores the player controls and cursor.
An interrupted opening finishes safely. The existing key, first gate, guard-room
clues, traps and wooden barricades remain independent.

PROGRESS
Progress is for the current Play session, like the existing castle puzzles.
A new Play session starts with a locked cell; no persistent save system is added.
