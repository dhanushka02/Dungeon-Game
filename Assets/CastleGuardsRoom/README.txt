CASTLE INTERIOR — THE ABANDONED GUARDS' ROOM

Scene group: Castle_GuardsRoom
The room occupies the bottom-left room, around the central stone enclosure.
The large far storeroom remains clear for the planned son rescue.

INTERACTIONS
Aim at a clue within 2.4 metres and press E. Readable panels close with E,
Escape, or when you move away. Furniture and stone walls block interactions.

- Guard orders: on the dining table, beside the open ledger and half-finished
  meal. The orders explain where the son was moved and point to the desk.
- Prisoner corner: inspect the bedding near the loose rope and empty bowl.
- Desk drawer: aim at its front handle and press E. It slides open once.
- Marked map: look down into the open drawer and press E to inspect the route.
  The large reader diagram identifies the guards' room, two barricades and
  far storeroom.
- Optional keepsake: a family drawing peeks from under the bedding. E collects
  it once; the room objective records that it is safely kept. It does not
  block main progression. CastleGuardsRoom.KeepsakeCollected exposes the flag
  for a future reunion interaction; no rescue NPC or ending is added here.

DECOR
Table, stools/chair, mugs, candle and meal; open supply cupboard with books
and bag; weapon rack and sword; scattered papers; mattress, blanket, pillow,
rope and bowl. New material copies keep props legible under the existing
castle lighting without changing shared materials elsewhere.

REMOVE OR DISABLE
Disable Castle_GuardsRoom to hide the entire addition, including UI and props.
Delete that root to remove it from the scene. Existing walls, barrels, player,
keys, gates and torch puzzle remain independent. Progress lasts for the current
Play session and resets when Play mode restarts, matching existing puzzles.

ASSETS
Existing BrokenVector and Synty props are reused from the project. Paper/ink,
clue graphics, furniture additions and the route diagram are original geometry.
PaperRustle.wav is an original synthesized effect. The drawer
uses Chest Open 1 from the already installed Free Fantasy SFX Pack By TomMusic.
No new packages were installed.
